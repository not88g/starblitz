
# 📘 STARBLITZ — Detailed Changelog (EN)

--- 

## 🔸 Debut / v1 / v1.1
**Features:**
- Game prototype.
- Player is a white square.
- Basic upward shooting.
- One type of enemy falling from top.
- Score increases when destroying enemies.

**Bug Fixes:**
- None (first version).

---

## 🔹 v1.3
**Features:**
- Background stars added, moving downward.

**Bug Fixes:**
- None.

---

## 🔹 v1.4
**Features:**
- Logo enlarged.
- Bottom text added: `v1.2 - made by 88g`.

**Bug Fixes:**
- None.

---

## 🔹 v1.5
**Features:**
- Player dies upon enemy collision.
- `GAME OVER` screen + `press Z to retry` text appears.

**Bug Fixes:**
- None.

---

## 🔹 v1.6
**Features:**
- Game Over triggered if enemies reach bottom.
- `GAME OVER` text colored red.

**Bug Fixes:**
- Fixed: enemies no longer fall through without consequence.

---

## 🔹 v1.7
**Features:**
- Background switched to fully black (`color 0`).

**Bug Fixes:**
- Removed visual noise.

---

## 🔹 v1.8
**Features:**
- New dark blue background (`color 1`).

**Bug Fixes:**
- Fixed blurry effects from previous version.

---

## 🔹 v1.9
**Features:**
- Sprites:
  - Player: `sprite 00`
  - Enemy: `sprite 01`

**Bug Fixes:**
- Removed temporary geometric shapes.

---

## 🔹 v2.0
**Features:**
- Enemies flipped 180° (flip_x and flip_y).

**Bug Fixes:**
- Enemies no longer look flat or misaligned.

---

## 🔹 v2.1
**Features:**
- Boss appears every 25 points (double size).
- Snowflake added next to logo on title screen.
- Version text: `v2.0`.

**Bug Fixes:**
- Boss scoring logic fixed.

---

## 🔹 v2.2
**Features:**
- Controls:
  - Arrow keys + WASD for movement.
  - Z, X, and Left Mouse Click for shooting.
- Step-based bullet animation.
- Enemy speed increases every 10 points.
- Restricted enemy spawn to one side at a time.

**Bug Fixes:**
- Removed dual-side enemy spawn bug.

---

## 🔹 v2.3
**Features:**
- Enlarged bullets.
- +1 life every 15 points.
- Victory screen at 9999 points: `YOU WON`, `thanks for playing`.

**Bug Fixes:**
- Hitboxes corrected (no more ghost hits).

---

## ✅ Stable v1.0
**Features:**
- Removed "Beta" from logo.
- Bottom text updated: `Stable - v1.0 | made by 88g`.
- Final title screen layout.
- End screen after winning the game.

**Bug Fixes:**
- Final polish and code cleanup.
